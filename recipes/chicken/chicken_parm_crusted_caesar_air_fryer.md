# Parmesan Crusted Chicken Caesar (Air Fryer)

Chicken parm without the sauce: thin cutlets in a half-panko, half-Parm crust, air fried until golden, then sliced over Caesar salad. Closer to a chicken Milanese than a true parm. Makes 4 cutlets from 2 breasts.

## Ingredients

**Chicken**
- 2 chicken breasts, each sliced in half lengthwise into 2 cutlets (4 total)
- Salt

**Breading**
- ½ cup flour, seasoned with salt and black pepper
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup panko
- 1 cup finely grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Cooking spray or oil spray

**Caesar**
- Romaine, chopped
- Bottled Caesar dressing
- Shaved Parmesan
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- Lemon wedges

**Equipment**
- Air fryer
- Wire rack
- Meat thermometer

## Steps

1. **Cut the cutlets:** Slice each breast in half lengthwise into thin cutlets, or pound to about ½". Salt them and let them sit 15–30 min if you have time.
2. **Set up the station:** Three shallow bowls: seasoned flour, beaten egg, then panko mixed with the Parm, garlic powder and lemon zest.
3. **Dredge:** Flour, then egg, then the panko-Parm. Press the crumbs on firmly. Rest the cutlets on a rack for about 10 min so the coating sets.
4. **Spray:** Right before cooking, spray both sides of each cutlet generously with oil.
5. **Air fry:** 400 °F for 10–12 min, flipping halfway and spraying the second side again if it looks dry. Start checking at 10 min and pull at 165 °F.
6. **Batch:** Two cutlets at a time, no overlapping. Keep the first batch warm on a rack in a low oven (about 200 °F) while the second cooks.
7. **Salad:** Toss the romaine with the bottled dressing. Top with shaved Parm and a good crack of black pepper.
8. **Serve:** Slice the chicken, lay it over the salad and finish with a squeeze of lemon.

## Nuances & Tips

- **Flour first, not egg:** The flour dries the surface so the egg sticks, and the egg holds the crumbs. Egg straight onto raw chicken slides off.
- **Load up the Parm:** With no sauce or melted cheese on top, the crust carries all the flavour. Half panko, half Parm is the point.
- **No need to toast the panko:** Air fryer heat comes mostly from the top, so untoasted panko can stay a bit pale on the bottom and edges. A generous spray on both sides plus the flip fixes that, and the Parm helps it brown.
- **How much breading:** 1 cup panko plus 1 cup Parm is plenty for 4 cutlets. If you run short, top up with plain panko.
- **Thin cutlets cook fast:** Check early. 10–12 min is a guide; 165 °F is the rule.
- **Rack, not paper towels:** Keeps the bottoms crisp while they wait.
- **Fancier bottled dressing:** Extra shaved Parm and plenty of black pepper on top make bottled Caesar taste homemade.

## Next Steps

Ideas for a crisper crust and a fuller version of the dish.

### 1. Toast the panko first
- **What:** Heat 1½ tbsp oil over medium heat until it shimmers (about 30 sec, no smoke). Add the 1 cup panko and stir constantly 2–3 min until light golden. Cool a minute before mixing in the Parm, or the cheese clumps.
- **Why:** More even golden colour, especially on the bottoms and edges.
- **Limit:** If it browns in under a minute, the heat is too high. It goes from golden to burnt fast.

### 2. Make it a real chicken parm
- **What:** After the cutlets cook, add a spoon of marinara (not over the edges) and low-moisture mozzarella, then air fry another 2–3 min until melted. The Marcella sauce from `noodles/spaghetti_meatballs_marcella.md` works here; start it about 45 min ahead.
- **Why:** The classic version, with the same crisp air fryer crust.

### 3. Homemade Caesar
- **What:** Swap the bottled dressing for a homemade one.
- **Why:** Brighter, more garlicky and lemony than bottled.
