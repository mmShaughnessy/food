# Blues Hog Chicken with Garlic Spinach and Roasted Peppers (Air Fryer)

A healthy meal-prep lunch: air-fried chicken breast with Blues Hog seasoning, garlic sautéed spinach and oven-roasted peppers. The chicken comes out by temperature, not time. Makes 3 portions.

## Ingredients

**Chicken**
- 3 chicken breasts (about 1 per portion)
- Blues Hog seasoning, to coat

**Roasted peppers**
- 2–3 bell peppers (red, yellow or orange are sweetest)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Pinch of salt and black pepper
- Optional: garlic powder or smoked paprika

**Garlic spinach**
- 10–12 oz (300–340 g) fresh spinach (one large bag or tub)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2–3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced or minced
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: squeeze of lemon, pinch of red pepper flakes

**Equipment**
- Air fryer
- Bluetooth meat thermometers

## Steps

1. **Peppers:** Heat the oven to 425 °F. Cut the peppers into strips, removing seeds and white ribs. Toss with oil, salt, pepper and any spices, and spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
2. **Roast:** Roast 20–25 min, flipping halfway, until soft with charred edges.
3. **Chicken:** Coat the breasts in Blues Hog seasoning and put a Bluetooth thermometer in the thickest part of each.
4. **Air fry:** Cook at 400 °F. Plan on about 25 min, but go by temperature and pull each breast at 165–170 °F.
5. **Spinach:** Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the garlic (and pepper flakes) and stir ~30 sec until fragrant, not browned.
6. **Wilt:** Add the spinach a big handful at a time, tossing with tongs as each batch wilts. Cook 2–3 min total, until just wilted and bright green. Season with salt and pepper, and add lemon off the heat.
7. **Drain:** Drain the spinach as well as possible before it goes into containers.
8. **Portion:** Slice the chicken and split everything across 3 containers.

## Nuances & Tips

- **Temperature beats time:** The 25 min is only a guide. The thermometers tell you when each breast is done, so timing stops mattering.
- **Spinach shrinks a lot:** 10–12 oz raw cooks down to about 1 cup total, which is right for 3 cooked portions.
- **Draining the spinach:** This was the key meal-prep discovery. Excess liquid makes the containers watery. Press it in a sieve with the back of a spoon for even drier spinach.
- **Garlic:** Keep it moving and pull it before it browns, or it goes bitter.
- **Single layer for the peppers:** Crowded peppers steam instead of roast.
- **Why the combo works:** Lean protein, fibre, iron and vitamins A and C. The vitamin C in the peppers helps you absorb the iron in the spinach. The sweet, smoky Blues Hog goes well with the roasted peppers.
- **Storage:** Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge. Store the spinach a bit apart from the chicken so it doesn't go soggy.

## Next Steps

Ideas for juicier chicken and better-holding meal prep.

### 1. Pull the chicken a little earlier
- **What:** Take the breasts out at around 160 °F and rest 5 min.
- **Why:** Carryover cooking takes it up a few degrees while it rests, so it finishes safely but stays juicier.
- **Food safety:** Check it reaches 165 °F after resting.

### 2. Paper towel in the containers
- **What:** Put a folded paper towel under the spinach in each container.
- **Why:** Soaks up any liquid that leaks out over the days.

### 3. Smoky peeled peppers
- **What:** Roast whole peppers at 450 °F for 30–40 min, turning until blackened. Cover in a bowl for 10 min, then peel and slice.
- **Why:** Silkier and smokier than strips. Jarred roasted red peppers (drained and patted dry) are the shortcut.

### 4. Make it more filling
- **What:** Add a small portion of quinoa, brown rice or chickpeas, or a sprinkle of feta, goat cheese or toasted pine nuts.
- **Why:** More staying power for lunch without much extra effort.
