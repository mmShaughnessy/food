# One-Pan Turkey Enchilada Skillet

A lighter, crowd-pleasing take on enchiladas without the calorie-heavy stack of tortillas. Lean ground turkey, bell peppers, black beans, corn and rice all cook together in one pan with red enchilada sauce and fire-roasted tomatoes, then finish under a layer of melted cheese and fresh toppings. Serves 4–6. About 35 minutes.

## Ingredients

**Turkey & veg**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb (~450 g) lean ground turkey (93/7)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1–2 bell peppers (red, yellow or orange), diced
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced

**Spices**
- 1½ tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Kosher salt and black pepper

**Skillet base**
- 1 cup (~200 g) long-grain white rice, rinsed well
- 1 can (10 oz / ~280 g) red enchilada sauce (mild or medium)
- 1 can (14.5 oz / ~410 g) fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1 cup (~240 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 can (15 oz / ~425 g) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup (~150 g) frozen or canned corn

**To finish**
- ¾–1 cup (~85–115 g) Monterey Jack or Mexican blend cheese, shredded
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Diced avocado
- Plain Greek yogurt (in place of sour cream)
- Optional: pickled jalapeños, hot sauce, small handful of crushed tortilla chips

**Equipment**
- Large, deep skillet with a tight-fitting lid (12" ideal)

## Steps

1. **Prep first:** Dice the onion and bell peppers, mince the garlic, rinse the rice well in a fine sieve, drain and rinse the black beans, and open the cans.
2. **Brown the turkey & veg:** Heat the olive oil in a large, deep skillet over medium-high. Add the ground turkey, onion, bell peppers and a generous pinch of salt. Cook 6–8 min, breaking up the turkey, until browned and the veggies start to soften.
3. **Bloom the spices:** Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika and oregano. Cook ~1 min until fragrant, stirring so the garlic doesn't burn.
4. **Add the skillet base:** Stir in the rinsed rice, red enchilada sauce, fire-roasted tomatoes (with juices), chicken broth, black beans and corn. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
5. **Covered simmer:** Bring to a boil, give it one good stir, cover tightly and drop the heat to low. Simmer undisturbed for 18–20 min until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
6. **Check & rest:** Peek at 18 min—if the rice still has bite and the pan looks dry, splash in ¼ cup broth or water and give it 3–5 more min covered.
7. **Melt the cheese:** Turn off the heat, fluff gently, and scatter the shredded cheese over the top. Re-cover for 2–3 min until melted.
8. **Finish & serve:** Squeeze fresh lime over the skillet and top with cilantro. Serve straight from the pan with avocado, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and any optional heat or crushed chips at the table.

## Nuances & Tips

- **93/7 turkey, not 99% fat-free:** Ultra-lean turkey breast dries out fast in a skillet. 93/7 stays tender while keeping the dish light.
- **Rinse the rice well:** Washing off the surface starch keeps the skillet fluffy instead of gummy once the enchilada sauce thickens.
- **Tight lid matters:** Steam is what cooks the rice evenly. If your lid is loose, lay a piece of foil under it before putting it on, and keep an eye on the liquid around the 15 min mark.
- **Don't keep lifting the lid:** Every peek lets out steam and adds minutes to the rice cook time. Wait until 18 min to check.
- **Keep the heat low during the simmer:** Tomato and enchilada sauce scorch quickly on the bottom of a pan once the rice absorbs the broth.
- **Greek yogurt swap:** Plain 2% or whole-milk Greek yogurt gives the same cool, tangy richness as sour cream with more protein and less fat.
- **Dialing the heat:** Use mild enchilada sauce to keep the whole pan kid-friendly, then let adults add jalapeños or hot sauce to their bowls.
- **Leftovers:** Keeps 4 days in the fridge and reheats well with a small splash of water or broth. Great for lunch bowls or stuffed into a single low-carb wrap.

## Next Steps

Ideas for tweaking the macros, flavour, or finish.

### 1. Half rice, half cauliflower rice
- **What:** Cut the white rice to ½ cup (and chicken broth to ½ cup), then fold in 2 cups riced cauliflower during the last 5 min of cooking.
- **Why:** Trims calories and carbs further while keeping the same cozy rice-skillet texture.

### 2. Broil the cheesy top
- **What:** If using an oven-safe skillet (cast iron or stainless steel), put the cheese-topped pan under the broiler for 2–3 min instead of melting under the lid.
- **Why:** Bubbly, browned cheese edges that feel even closer to baked enchiladas.

### 3. Salsa verde version
- **What:** Swap the red enchilada sauce and diced tomatoes for a 16 oz jar of salsa verde plus a small can of diced green chiles, and top with pepper jack.
- **Why:** Brighter, tangier tomatillo riff using the exact same one-pan method.

### 4. Homemade red enchilada sauce
- **What:** Whisk 2 tbsp tomato paste, 2 tbsp chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and a little flour in oil, then simmer 10 min with 2 cups chicken broth.
- **Why:** Deeper, less metallic flavour than canned sauce, with full control over salt and heat.

### 5. Baked corn-tortilla strips
- **What:** Slice 2–3 thin corn tortillas into strips, mist with oil spray and salt, and air fry at 400 °F for 4–5 min until crisp.
- **Why:** Gives everyone real tortilla crunch on top for a fraction of the calories of rolled enchiladas.
